We exist at the intersection of music, art, and mental wellness. From live shows and open mics to creative collaborations and community experiences — this is a space for people who create from the heart.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Petrichor?

A community where music, art, and mental health come together — through open mics, live shows, and creative collaborations. A space for people who create from the heart.

Do I need experience to perform?

Not at all. First-timers are as welcome as seasoned performers — Petrichor is built for artists finding their voice, not just showing it off.

Is there a cost to join or perform?

Joining the community and applying to perform is free. Just fill out the artist form and we'll reach out with the details.

What kinds of acts can I bring?

Singing, storytelling, stand-up, spoken word, bands, visual art — if it's honest self-expression, it belongs on our stage.
